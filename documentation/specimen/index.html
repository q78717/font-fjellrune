Elder Futhark & Nordic Runes

Fjellrune includes native support for the Unicode Runic block (U+16A0–U+16F0), drawn with the same blade-cut geometry and axe-nicked staves as the Latin alphabet.

Waterfall

The system

Blade-cut geometry

Every glyph is built from straight polygonal segments only — no Bézier curves. Terminals are chamfered at an axe-cut angle, and vertices are mitre-joined with automatic beveling so sharp apexes never spike.

The axe nick

Every stroke 330 units or longer receives a triangular bite (28% of the stroke width deep) along its leading edge, giving staves and diagonals the weathered look of carved wood and runestones.

Faceted lowercase

Lowercase letters sit on a 480-unit x-height with hexagonal six-sided bowls and 730-unit ascenders, pairing naturally with the 700-unit capitals for titles, jerseys, and posters.

Optical balance & kerning

Diagonals are thinned by 9% relative to vertical stems. Class kerning (7,822 pairs) is computed from full glyph profiles across all base and accented letters.
